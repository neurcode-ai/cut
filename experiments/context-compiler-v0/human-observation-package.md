## Aggregate gates

All measurements must come from participant behavior:

- At least 70% of compiler-proposed items retained.
- Fewer than 15% substantial context addback.
- At least four of five developers report that the proposal saved time.
- Zero publication of material a participant did not understand.
- Report how many participants rechecked the full change.

If the protocol is ever run for a future preregistered implementation, retain
the raw observation forms privately with appropriate consent and commit only
source-free aggregate counts.
