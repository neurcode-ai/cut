# Community conduct

Participate with respect, technical honesty, and care for other people's
privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and
publication of private material are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or
escalate credible safety concerns. Reports may be sent privately to
`security@neurcode.com`. Include only the minimum information needed to
investigate.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when they
contain sensitive information, and open to review by a maintainer who was not
the original decision-maker.
